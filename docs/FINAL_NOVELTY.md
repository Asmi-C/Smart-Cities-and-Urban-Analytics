# Candidate technical contribution

**One-sentence method:** We propose an auditable spatially cross-fitted intervention screening protocol that reports Pareto severity, rank acceptability under explicit preference uncertainty, and geographic predictive uncertainty as separate decision dimensions, retaining a transparent 'needs validation' option.

This is an **architecture-level method proposal** combining established tools, not a proven first-in-literature algorithm. Its value must be established empirically against simpler ranking and prioritization methods.

1. A validation stress test across randomized, geographic-block, contiguous-region and buffered holdouts, plus spatial residual diagnostics.
2. Explicit feature-group and spatial KNN comparisons, with uncertainty on model differences.
3. Multiobjective Pareto and rank-acceptability analysis over heat, service access and blue-green deficits, rather than merely a point-weighted score.
4. Separate uncertainty flags, intervention types and population-exposure context, designed to avoid claiming unsupported causal outcomes.

The contribution remains conditional on sensitivity, replication and stakeholder assessment.

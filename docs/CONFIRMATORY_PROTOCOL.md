# Round-9 endpoint and anti-selection protocol

1. Development material is the entire already-examined 15-date corpus. A model tuned or selected here is **not** confirmatory.
2. **Exploratory joint geography/date primary endpoint:** geographic-block/date macro-mean anomaly R² (up to four quadrants x fifteen dates, with a predefined >=15-target-hex feasibility threshold), paired hybrid vs IDW historical-climatology baseline. Secondary RMSE, hotspot AUROC, residual Moran across k=4/8/12 and a distance band. Geographic blocks are frozen at geographic centroid median x/y, no boundary adjustments based on LST. Inference should aggregate within year/date and spatial block; do not treat hexes as independent replications. **Observed:** quadrant 3 had no eligible date folds and quadrant 1 had only ten, yielding 40 folds in three geographic quadrants. Report these omissions, never imply 60 folds.
3. No held-date or held-quadrant LST is permitted in RF fitting, source residual correction, neighbor site history or model selection. Target spectra may be used for unlabeled z-score alignment (a transductive, not inductive setting). Unknown-location history defaults to source-site-neighbor interpolation, **never** zero without exposing the fallback.
4. A future independent city or untouched-date cohort must be locked separately, with acquired scene metadata and predefined region/sensor/QA rules, *before* inspecting labels, to support final confirmatory superiority claims.
5. Report each negative result, confidence intervals at year-group level if feasible, inability to score AUROC when a small fold lacks both classes, and failure to clear any reviewer gate.

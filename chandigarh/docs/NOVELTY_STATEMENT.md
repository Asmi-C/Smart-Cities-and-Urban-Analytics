# Specific novelty claim (provisional)

We develop a reproducible NKDA-scale evaluation protocol combining 1-km-block spatial cross-validation of satellite-derived thermal patterns with weight-perturbation stability mapping for intervention screening. In contrast with local descriptive UHI/LULC studies, the empirical contribution is the **validation and stability assessment of urban priority rankings**, not a claim to have invented RF, Moran's I, or multi-criteria indices.

This combined protocol requires a more complete literature review before claiming it is globally novel. Current intervention categories remain heuristic and unvalidated. The work is a credible research prototype but is not yet submission-ready.

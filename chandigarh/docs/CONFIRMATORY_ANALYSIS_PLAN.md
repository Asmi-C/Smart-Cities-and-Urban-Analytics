# Future untouched confirmation, frozen before any new label evaluation

Source cohort: the 15 Landsat-8 Collection-2 Level-2 scenes already analyzed in Rounds 7–9. No new acquisition is yet designated or scored as a truly untouched test. Candidate scenes, if obtained, must be selected by date (Feb15–May15), WRS path/row (138/045), sensor Level-2 processing availability, predeclared cloud <=25% and >=180 clear spectrally valid hexes, without examining model outcomes. New years and Landsat 9 may require predeclared harmonization; intersensor comparability must be audited rather than assumed. Dates excluded for missing coverage must remain in the manifest.

**Models frozen:** feature-standardized Ridge, RandomForestRegressor 45 trees/min_samples_leaf5/max_features 0.8, HistGradientBoosting max_iter80/min_samples_leaf10, RF with static standardized projected x/y; random_state42 where applicable. No site-history residual correction as a proposed winner.

**Primary endpoint:** D2-to-D3 macro-anomaly-R² optimism, and whether the best D2 model differs from the best D3 model. Paired secondary RMSE, hotspot AUROC, residual Moran and coverage. Geographic quadrants from original global centroid medians, >=15 target hex threshold. Test acquisition labels are wholly excluded from model fitting and from any data-dependent tuning or anchor selection; target acquisition spectra can be used only for unlabeled normalization.

**Inference:** aggregate within acquisitions/calendar years and geographic blocks, report confidence intervals and signs, avoid hex-level pseudoreplication. Once any confirmatory outcomes are observed, do not tune and re-use those acquisitions as untouched again. If independent candidates cannot be downloaded, status remains NOT CONFIRMED.

# Landsat-8 acquisition validity and unresolved physical checks

**Source/product:** USGS Landsat 8 Collection 2 Level 2 Surface Temperature product (`ST_B10` in ST source, signed through Planetary Computer), WRS path/row 138/045. No Landsat-9 or mixed-sensor comparison has been executed.

**Code-backed scaling:** `external_case/temporal_landsat.py` converts ST DN via `DN * 0.00341802 + 149.0 - 273.15` to °C. QA_PIXEL bit flags for fill, dilated cloud, cirrus, cloud, shadow and snow are masked before aggregation. NDVI, NDBI and MNDWI use corresponding Landsat 8 surface reflectance inputs. The Landsat L2 surface-temperature product inherits USGS emissivity/retrieval assumptions and is not independent in-situ thermal truth.

**Selection and support:** 695 WRS138045 metadata candidates screened with scene cloud <=25%, Feb15–May15 within 2018–2026, at most two sufficiently separated scenes per year with prior six retained. 16 selected; 15 pass >=180 finite thermal/spectral hex observations (189–311); excluded 2025-05-06 (28 eligible hexes). Scene item IDs and cloud values: `data/acquisition_manifest_final.csv`. Harmonized date-specific pixel support and `n_clear_pixels` per hex: `external_case/data/landsat_hex_LC08_YYYYMMDD.csv`. Geographic common support: 180 identical hexes across 15 dates. Acquisition context: `data/acquisition_context_final.csv` (some meteorological fields unavailable).

**Empirical physical-consistency evidence:** The 15-date site climatology averages anomaly R² 0.723 while raw spectral RF averages 0.450, and common-support 180-site historical climatology reaches R² 0.745 vs normalized RF 0.632; this makes changing cloud masks an insufficient explanation of the persistent-location result. Held-date and year holdouts establish temporal *retrospective* robustness, not field-calibrated physical temperature transfer. Weather-regressed absolute LST mean R² stays negative (-0.441), and scene-mean centering uses the evaluation acquisition's true LST mean only for target construction.

**Still unvalidated:** independent in-situ LST or air-temperature comparison, sensor-specific systematic error audit, optical/thermal viewing-geometry correction, per-date emissivity error distribution, fully harmonized cross-sensor transfer and new-city replication. The source products and scene metadata can be audited and reacquired through the included scripts. No physical claim is upgraded until these checks exist.

**Screening note:** same Landsat product is necessary but not sufficient evidence of physical comparability. Acquisition timing, cloud-clear support and meteorological conditions vary across years; reported models are best understood as thermal-pattern monitoring under acquisition shift.
